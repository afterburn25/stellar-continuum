# Stellar Continuum

Public development repository for **Stellar Continuum**, an original realism-first real-time space civilization strategy game.

**Naming status:** Stellar Continuum is the canonical working title. Commercial trademark/domain clearance is still pending; see [`docs/BRANDING.md`](docs/BRANDING.md).

## Current development

Gameplay version: `0.1.1 Alpha` (technical SemVer: `0.1.1-alpha`)

Engine: Godot 4.7.2 .NET / C# (`net8.0`)

New campaigns begin on **January 1, 2050** with the player and normal major civilizations pre-warp. A small number of distant old powers begin already spacefaring but are non-expansionist and neutral unless provoked.

The current gameplay code is still a prototype. The canonical design direction is broader: a human-like 2050 start includes substantial orbital infrastructure, a permanent lunar presence, a young Mars colony, and meaningful solar-system development before practical interstellar expansion.

## Player and Developer modes

Development now targets one full game with separate Player and Developer campaigns.
Player uses ordinary rules and knowledge. Developer adds explicit testing tools,
a reproducible world seed and a 24x clock, with separate saves and a persistent
tools-used marker. See [GAME_MODES.md](docs/GAME_MODES.md) for switching, saves and
the remaining full-game priorities. This is an unfinished development build.

Graphical navigation connects the Milky Way overview, stellar region, system,
focused planet and a freely navigable 3D colony surface. See
[map and surface controls](docs/CINEMATIC_MAP_AND_SURFACE.md).

## Adaptive Research — canonical direction

The Alpha runtime uses the Adaptive Research campaign, with visible eligibility,
laboratory allocation, funding commitments and timed progress in the research workspace.

Stellar Continuum uses an **Adaptive Research System**:

- the universe contains a broad hidden Technology Possibility Graph
- the player never sees the complete future graph
- each civilization sees only technologies it currently understands or has reason/evidence to investigate
- branches emerge from needs, basic science, environment, warfare, discoveries, alien contact, captured technology, and institutional history
- similar capabilities can be reached through different technological implementations
- the same species can develop radically different research trees in different campaigns
- foreign technology is not automatically usable or instantly unlocked

The player-facing research economy is **RP + Pressure + Labs**:

- **Research Points (RP)** — generated by Effective Research Labs and applied to directed projects
- **Research Pressure** — contextual need/evidence; only explicitly configured technologies are hard-gated by pressure
- **Effective Research Labs** — physical scientific capacity; every directed project requires a minimum allocation

Early civilizations formally direct one major research program while unassigned laboratories continue basic/exploratory science. Parallel directed research is unlocked by the evolving tree itself:

- **Coordinated Research Networks** → 2 directed programs
- **Distributed Scientific Portfolios** → up to 4
- **Autonomous Research Portfolios** → no artificial slot ceiling; available lab capacity becomes the practical limit

The current public seed design catalog contains **330 normal/public possibilities across 20 domains**, **59 Research Pressure types**, and **15 alternative-solution sets**. It lives under [`data/research/v1/`](data/research/v1/) and is structurally validated in CI.

## Current alpha gameplay

The alpha loop uses adaptive capability costs and timers for research, construction,
production, fleet travel and colony growth. The current campaign supports the mouse-driven
map, diplomacy workspace, colony surface and ordinary Player/Developer save separation;
the full continuous expedition and pacing review remain in progress.

## Current controls and saves

Player interaction is mouse-first through the adaptive map, sidebar, diplomacy and colony
surfaces; keyboard shortcuts remain available for development and accessibility checks. See
[map and surface controls](docs/CINEMATIC_MAP_AND_SURFACE.md) and [game modes](docs/GAME_MODES.md).
Current save compatibility is v16/v17, with mode-separated Player and Developer saves.
