# Alternative Biochemistry & Exotic Biospheres

Stellar Continuum assumes **carbon + water is likely to be the most common naturally evolved complex-life family**, but it is not universal.

The research system therefore treats biology as a set of **composable biochemical/environmental dimensions**, not as a set of named race classes.

## Core rule

A species does not receive a separate technology tree because it is human, aquatic, silicon-centered, ammonia-based, or synthetic.

Instead, a population has biological facts such as:

- metabolic or synthetic;
- carbon-centered or silicon-centered/mixed chemistry;
- dominant solvent/environment;
- active temperature regime;
- mineral/inorganic structural dependence;
- liquid-medium dependence;
- gravity/pressure/environmental sensitivities.

Those facts determine which specific biochemical solutions are applicable. The same shared Technology Possibility Graph still drives every civilization.

## Public applicability dimensions

### Generic metabolism

`metabolic_biology` means the population is biological and needs metabolism/homeostasis.

It intentionally does **not** mean terrestrial carbon-water biology.

### Molecular substrate

Current public normal traits include:

- `carbon_centered_biochemistry`
- `silicon_centered_biochemistry`

Silicon-centered life is speculative and intended to be rare. It should be designed as chemically constrained alternative biology rather than a fantasy “rock race.”

A silicon-centered species does not automatically tolerate every high temperature, radiation field, or pressure.

### Dominant solvent

Current public normal traits include:

- `water_solvent_biology`
- `ammonia_rich_biology`
- `hydrocarbon_solvent_biology`

Solvent and molecular substrate are separate dimensions. A carbon-centered organism can use a water, ammonia-rich, or cryogenic hydrocarbon environment depending on its evolved chemistry.

### Temperature regime

`cryogenic_biology` describes biology whose normal active state is extremely cold by terrestrial-water standards.

Cold is not automatically a penalty for that species; **warm terrestrial conditions may be hostile to it instead**.

### Structural growth

`mineral_structural_biology` describes organisms that rely heavily on biologically controlled mineral/inorganic deposition in living structure.

This can exist with or without silicon-centered metabolic chemistry.

### Native medium

`liquid_medium_native` describes a population evolved primarily to live/function in a liquid medium. This is independent of solvent chemistry and can affect habitat, pressure, locomotion, interfaces, and equipment.

## Why these are traits instead of race IDs

The game can support combinations we did not hand-author as races.

Examples:

- carbon + water + liquid-medium native;
- carbon + ammonia-rich solvent;
- carbon + hydrocarbon solvent + cryogenic active biology;
- silicon-centered + mineral-structural biology;
- silicon-centered biology that still depends on a particular liquid solvent;
- a multispecies civilization containing several incompatible biochemical populations at once.

The research engine asks whether a **population context** has the required traits. It never asks whether the player selected “Race #7.”

## New public research domain

Milestone #9 expands the public graph with **Alternative Biochemistry & Exotic Biospheres**.

The first seed contains 30 normal/public possibilities.

### Shared alternative-biochemistry foundations

- Alternative-Solvent Biochemistry Modeling
- Extreme-Biochemistry Homeostasis
- Biochemical Feedstock Engineering
- Extreme-Biochemistry Containment Engineering

These are broad enough to be relevant to many metabolic civilizations that encounter unusual environmental or foreign-biological problems.

### Ammonia-rich biological lineage

Examples include:

- ammonia-solvent homeostasis;
- closed-loop ammonia recycling;
- compatible nutrient synthesis;
- ammonia bioregenerative life support;
- ammonia bioindustry;
- ammonia ecosystem stabilization.

These nodes require the relevant ammonia-rich biological context and remain invisible to incompatible populations unless comparative/foreign science creates a different route to awareness.

### Cryogenic hydrocarbon biological lineage

Examples include:

- cryogenic hydrocarbon homeostasis;
- cryogenic biological membrane engineering;
- hydrocarbon-biosphere nutrient synthesis;
- cryogenic closed ecology;
- cryogenic bioreactor industry;
- cryogenic biosphere management.

These require both hydrocarbon-solvent and cryogenic biological applicability.

### Silicon-centered / mineral-structural lineage

Examples include:

- silicon-centered biochemical frameworks;
- silicate metabolic-cycle control;
- mineral nutrient processing;
- silicon-centered tissue repair;
- mineral-structural regeneration;
- mineral biofabrication;
- silicon-biochemistry closed life-support cycles;
- silicon biological computation.

This is intentionally **not** a universal superior-tech path. It creates different opportunities and dependencies.

For example, mineral feedstocks may be common in some environments yet complex biochemical fabrication may be difficult; terrestrial medicine may be useless; some foreign biological technologies may be impossible to operate directly.

### Cross-biochemistry science

After genuine alien biological evidence exists, civilizations can develop comparative capabilities such as:

- Cross-Solvent Comparative Biochemistry
- Xenonutrient Translation
- Cross-Biochemistry Interface Engineering
- Cross-Biochemistry Medicine
- Multi-Biochemistry Habitat Support
- Cross-Biochemistry Biofabrication

These branches require legitimate `alien_biology` evidence. They do not appear merely because the full catalog contains them.

## Existing Research Pressures are reused

We deliberately did **not** create a new global pressure for every unusual biological problem.

Existing causal pressures already express most needs:

- `food_shortage`
- `water_shortage`
- `resource_scarcity`
- `life_support_losses`
- `extreme_temperature`
- `high_pressure_environment`
- `oceanic_environment`
- `colony_isolation`
- `industrial_bottleneck`
- `alien_biology_encountered`
- `disease_burden`
- `ecological_damage`
- `foreign_technology_observed`

The rule is:

> Reuse the pressure that describes the actual problem. Do not create another meter just because the population solving it has exotic chemistry.

That keeps the pressure system understandable and bounded.

## Same strategic capabilities, different implementations

Alternative biochemistry plugs into existing cross-lineage capabilities.

Examples:

- ammonia bioregenerative support -> `long_duration_habitation`
- cryogenic closed ecology -> `long_duration_habitation`
- silicon life-support cycles -> `long_duration_habitation`
- ammonia/hydrocarbon/mineral nutrient systems -> `food_independence`
- advanced ammonia/cryogenic/mineral systems can contribute to industrial autonomy
- advanced biochemical ecosystem technologies can provide `biosphere_independence`

Therefore shipbuilding/colonization/logistics can ask:

> Does this population have Long-Duration Habitation?

without caring whether the solution is a human rotating habitat, torpor, ammonia ecology, cryogenic hydrocarbon ecology, silicon biochemical cycling, or a future foreign/hybrid solution.

## Research facilities

Alternative biochemistry uses modular research-facility extensions rather than generic research bonuses.

Current research-facing specialist institutions include:

- Alternative Biochemistry Institute
- Cryogenic Biochemistry Institute
- Mineral Biochemistry Institute
- Cross-Biochemistry Interface Laboratory

They provide actual experiment/containment/prototyping capabilities and Effective Research Lab capacity.

Missing a required physical biochemical environment can block an experimental/engineering stage with a clear reason rather than becoming an opaque `-50% research` modifier.

Construction/economy owns how these facilities are physically built and maintained.

## Starting scientific histories

Starting profile data is modular.

The core catalog still contains the original history fragments/reference starts, while supplemental biochemical catalogs add:

- ammonia-rich biochemical history;
- cryogenic hydrocarbon biochemical history;
- silicon-centered/mineral-structural history.

Reference starts demonstrate the architecture but are **not named playable races or future trees**.

The human-like reference is now explicitly:

- carbon-centered;
- water-solvent;
- gravity-sensitive;
- heritable genetic biology.

This prevents “human biology” from silently remaining the unmarked universal default.

## Foreign biological technology

Biochemical compatibility feeds into the existing foreign-technology model rather than creating a separate alien-tech minigame.

A foreign biological technology can therefore be:

- understandable but unusable;
- operable only in its origin species' environment;
- reproducible only with imported feedstock or biological cultures;
- adaptable into a native derivative;
- useful only as scientific evidence;
- economically valuable because another compatible civilization wants it.

This directly supports technology brokerage and the user's original design goal that **something useless to us can still be extremely valuable to someone else**.

## Scaling

The expanded public catalog is now designed around:

- **360 normal/public nodes**
- **21 domains**
- **59 Research Pressure types** (unchanged)
- **16 alternative-solution sets**
- **14 applicability traits**
- **9 evidence types**
- **36 knowledge fields**

The full graph remains static shared data. A civilization only stores/materializes its own small relevant subset.

Adding 30 nodes therefore does not mean adding 30 active runtime objects per civilization.

## Public secrecy

This domain contains only ordinary normal-public biochemical possibilities.

Rare secret life forms, secret artifact biology, exact generation probabilities, and intentionally undisclosed rare discovery chains remain outside the public graph/docs.
