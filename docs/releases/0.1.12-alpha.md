# Stellar Continuum 0.1.12 Alpha

- Star-map stars begin at one quarter of their previous diameter. Deeper, pointer-anchored zoom reveals the existing detailed stellar artwork without switching views. Solar-system star sizing is unchanged.
- Double-click an explored star to enter its system. The bottom context plate switches between the focused system and star map, identifies the empire/species or current system, and changes to gold while paused.
- A thin resource strip holds the separate play/pause and speed controls. Native play speeds run from 1x to 4x, including actual simulation timing.
- The right planet list shows owned worlds with their portraits and population. Clicking a world centers it in its system and opens planetary statistics and building management.
- The fleet, system and colony panels are more compact, with responsive layout anchored to a 1920x1080 reference and support for 1280x720 and larger displays.
- Connected system arrows retain their orange hover state during fleet refresh, depress while pressed, and navigate only on a matching mouse release. Cancelled presses do not navigate.
- Windowed mode restores a decorated, resizable window even after a maximized/fullscreen transition. Brief focus changes during display switching no longer immediately undo the selection. Borderless Fullscreen remains the default, and Keep/Revert protection remains available.

Existing saves remain supported. Galaxy generation, seeded stellar populations, observer visibility and research are carried forward. This release does not claim full visual parity with the earlier Godot game.
