# Diplomacy and Adaptive Research campaign integration contract

Status: source review and integration design. Low-level native Diplomacy state is implemented in gate068; integrated Diplomacy runtime and player-save parity remain open. Preserve the existing Godot/C# playable baseline and native legacy coordinator until the replacement composition passes actual-source comparisons.

## Authoritative campaign composition

The current player pipeline is `src/Game/Presentation/Main.CoreIntegration.cs`, not the isolated legacy coordinator alone. Rebuild requires a populated AdaptiveResearchCampaignState. Construction and shipbuilding use their Adaptive capability adapters; the strategic director receives the same shipbuilding adapter and DiplomacyStrategicKnowledgeProvider. A single DiplomacyCampaignRuntimeCoordinator owns its command gateway and hostility view; combat preview and issuance receive that exact view. The legacy research advancement flag is false, which also disables legacy science accrual.

For each accepted substep: advance the existing galaxy coordinator; on positive elapsed time record fleet sensor contacts in civilization-ID order; advance Adaptive Research with both elapsed days and the absolute end day; then pass this step's Exploration and Combat events to Diplomacy at the same end day. Diplomatic processing is Exploration contacts, Combat incidents, then maintenance. Do not reorder research ahead of the core economy/construction phase, or update diplomatic hostility midway through combat execution. Publish returned player events only after authoritative mutations. Autosave occurs after every bounded substep in the frame has completed. Developer fast-forward uses the same path in at most 0.25-day steps and writes one checkpoint after the command boundary.

The native replacement should own the mutable world, Adaptive campaign and diplomatic state in stable storage. Inject capability and observer-knowledge adapters through the existing typed coordinator configuration and strategic runtime, not substitute per-phase callbacks. Lambdas and borrowed views must reference stable owned storage, never a movable wrapper address. Preserve the existing legacy benchmark configuration for its retained fixtures. The integrated configuration must explicitly disable legacy research; enabling both research engines would double-advance science and create conflicting technology authority.

## Capability projections

`ConstructionCapabilities.cs`: orbital_industry accepts that capability or established orbital_manufacturing. warp_field_control, fusion_power, additive_manufacturing, interplanetary_trade_standards and closed_loop_recycling require established knowledge of that ID. Other requests use the exact capability flag. `ShipbuildingCapabilities.cs`: spacecraft construction accepts its capability, orbital_industry capability, or established orbital_manufacturing; experimental interstellar transit requires its exact capability; all remaining IDs use exact capability flags. These adapters retrieve the campaign civilization and preserve its missing-ID failure; legacy technology vectors must not become a fallback that bypasses Adaptive prerequisites.

## Diplomacy ownership and visibility

`DiplomacySystem.cs` owns directional contacts, canonical bilateral relationships, directional access, claims and claim responses, agreements, proposals, bounded history and monotonic IDs. Contacts and political relationships are different knowledge domains: an observation must not manufacture reciprocal awareness. Public DTOs and views own their contents; commands borrow one stable state. Snapshot sorting and insertion-order behavior are observable and must match the source where no explicit sort exists.

`BuildViewFor(observer)` exposes that observer's contacts, sorted by UTF-16 ordinal contact ID. Relationship, access, agreement and proposal visibility is filtered by that observer's identified targets. Claims require explicit KnownTo membership; a visible claim response is additionally restricted to its responder or claimant. History requires explicit audience membership. `DiplomacyStrategicKnowledgeProvider` consumes this view only, excludes self and sorts distinct identified targets. Sentiment is clamp(trust-hostility,-1,1), war is exact AtWar; every foreign military estimate remains unavailable with zero confidence. Do not infer intelligence, shared borders or military strength by scanning the galaxy.

`DiplomacyCombatHostilityView` is read-only: self, missing relationships, Unknown, Peace and Ceasefire are not hostile; Hostile and AtWar are. Preview and actual orders must share the same instance/state, including after load. A legal access denial is not a physical movement barrier: source transit authorization requires granted access and not AtWar but explicitly does not act as a force field.

## Event bridges and cadence

ExplorationDiplomacyBridge processes only FirstContact events in incoming order. Missing TargetCivilizationId is an authored failure, not an invitation to guess. It creates only the observer's contact `civilization:<target>`, with ContactEstablished/Active, confidence 1, communication false, event system and supplied tick. A later invalid event retains earlier successful changes.

CombatDiplomacyBridge ignores missing/self targets and events where the victim has not identified the attacker. It processes only EngagementStarted (SetHostile) and FleetDestroyed (a grievance of severity 1 with zero other impact axes). Per-salvo damage, retreats and end events do not create diplomatic history. Messages include the supplied system or unknown; authoritative attacker identity cannot leak into victim-visible history without attribution.

The integer clock uses 1000 ticks/day: reject negative/nonfinite days, floor(days*1000), saturate to int64 maximum. Whole-day conversion requires positive input and saturates before multiplication. Default maintenance reviews every day, stale threshold 90 days and proposal lifetime 30 days. Validate policy in interval/stale/lifetime order. Reset checks time, clears last-review to -1 and schedules now or a saturating future interval. ReviewIfDue initializes lazily, skips before next review and skips the already-reviewed tick; it ages contacts before expiring proposals and advances cadence only after both return. Large elapsed jumps run one bounded review, not one scan per missed tick. A partial failure can retain contact mutations while leaving cadence unchanged.

Runtime Reset derives tick and resets maintenance, then sets last-processed tick. Process rejects a regressing tick before consuming events; same-tick calls are allowed while maintenance remains idempotent. Last-processed advances only after both bridges and maintenance succeed. Cadence is transient and resets for current loaded time, optionally reviewing immediately; do not serialize a stale maintenance deadline as new campaign policy.

## Restoration and acceptance boundaries

`DiplomacyState.Restore` is a permissive low-level historical conversion: it truncates bounded collections, skips some invalid entries, clamps selected values, deduplicates audiences, restores canonical pairs and repairs ID counters. It is not equivalent to the strict player save path. `DiplomacySnapshotInvariantValidator` plus campaign cross-reference validation run before restoration in current player persistence. Keep these entry points separate and test both; never replace strict save validation with silent repair. The precise player wrapper17 / galaxy16 and Developer wrapper rules are in CAMPAIGN_PERSISTENCE_MIGRATION_CONTRACT.md.

Implementation order: state/DTOs and controlled commands with source fixtures; observer-safe views and command gateway; communication/claims/proposals and lifecycle services; strict snapshots; event/hostility/AI bridges and cadence; then stable integrated campaign composition. Acceptance needs full snapshots around ordered command sequences, state after partial failures, hidden-contact probes, exact counts at bounds, copy/move ownership, signed saturation boundaries, resumed campaign progression, and source/native equivalence with legacy advancement disabled. This review does not assert full command or invariant coverage: their individual implementations require bounded source review and retained fixtures before promotion.

## Current-save validation boundary

Source reviewed: `DiplomacySnapshotInvariantValidator.cs` (324 lines). This is a separate gate from permissive low-level Restore. Validate all eight top-level arrays for presence in source order before collection bounds or record checks. A future JSON/DTO boundary must retain missing/null presence or reject it at the equivalent validation stage; silently converting null into an empty vector would erase a required current-save failure. Valid native-owned vectors need no nullable interface, but parser and malformed-source probes must document this distinction.

Record validation order is contacts, relationships, directional access, claims, responses, agreements, proposals, history, then four next-ID counters. Preserve exact first failure and do not mutate inputs. Contacts build directional identified pairs; each canonical relationship requires at least one legitimate identified direction. Access, agreements and proposals must reference a relationship. Claims require sorted distinct nonnegative audiences including their claimant; responses require a known non-self responder and cannot predate the claim. Active agreements must be unique per pair/type; trade records require external terms. Pending proposals are bounded per canonical pair and cannot have resolved ticks; resolved proposals require valid chronology. History IDs increase in insertion order, audiences are sorted/distinct and include the primary civilization. Next IDs must be positive and strictly above every retained ID, without overflowing a max+1 calculation.

This validator verifies structure and contact knowledge, not existence in the current galaxy. Campaign persistence performs the subsequent galaxy-reference validation separately. Do not repair a rejected current save by routing it through tolerant Restore. Native overflow checks for low-level historical recovery remain explicitly different boundaries from C# unchecked counters.
