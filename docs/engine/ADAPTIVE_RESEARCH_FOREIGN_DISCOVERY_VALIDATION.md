# Adaptive Research foreign-discovery validation

Gate 059 ports the immutable foreign-awareness materialization policy and the
coordinator that delegates factual foreign-technology changes before bounded
research-graph reevaluation. The coordinator borrows stable Authority,
ForeignTechnologyRuntime, and discovery-catalog objects and owns no
per-civilization support.

The actual C# fixture records the complete discovery catalog, exact malformed
policy bytes and SHA-256 digests, scratch fingerprints before and after each
load, and complete core/foreign state around every typed command. Sequential
coverage includes all four delegation wrappers, direct reevaluation, evidence
and method candidates, first-reason retention on equal-rank evidence/method
ties, graph-depth ordering, protected Experimental/Mature/archive records, and
case-insensitive reopening of disproven archives while retaining total work.
An intentionally malformed in-memory rule proves factual assessment mutation
is retained when the subsequent reevaluation rejects an above-Investigable
target.

Native-only probes verify every borrowed rvalue constructor guard, coordinator
move composition, and ownership of reference/context/constraint views that
alias a foreign assessment replaced by the delegated factual call. Generic JSON
parser and operating-system diagnostics remain outside exact parity; authored
catalog validations and retained JSON-kind failures are exact.

The final fixture contains 18 sequential commands and 14 retained catalog
cases. Its SHA-256 is
`8DF43CBE4B49B82D2CACEC3B5989498702ABEAA433544BD4FB17A9DB23992F47`.

transcripts are `build-debug/compile.log`, `build-debug/run.log`,

returned exit 1 with exception, working-directory, research-root, and fixture
